# Initial edition — 10 October 2026

Seven games: Veloren, Unciv, 0 A.D., SuperTuxKart, Pioneer, Naev and Endless Sky. Seven Android apps: LocalSend, Fossify Gallery, AntennaPod, URLCheck, Material Files, HeliBoard and StreetComplete. All are existing products newly discovered by F7; none is presented as a recent first launch.

Six apps have developer-linked Google Play distribution; HeliBoard is F-Droid/official GitHub. Unciv and SuperTuxKart also have developer-linked Play listings. Fossify Gallery, HeliBoard and StreetComplete have unknown monetary prices; no free claim is inferred from a software license.

Every public product page carries primary evidence, the access date and artwork acknowledgments. Original bytes and source/permission URLs are recorded in `initial-assets.json`. No APKs or Google Play API were used. Source limitations and disabled routes are in `SOURCES.md`.

Artwork checks were separate from code licensing. Veloren, 0 A.D., Fossify and AntennaPod provide specific editorial/press permissions. Unciv's banner and components, SuperTuxKart's named logo, Pioneer's art assignment, Naev's original media and HeliBoard's explicitly licensed icon have asset grants and credits. Endless Sky's machine-readable all-file grant covers its icons directory, with asset exceptions checked. LocalSend and Material Files use original owner artwork tracked within the licensed work, with no contrary asset exception found in examined notices. StreetComplete's icon has its own GPLv2 assignment and editable-original evidence, distinct from the app's broad code license. Full GPL/Apache notices accompany applicable local files.

URLCheck credits TrianguloY visibly in Artwork acknowledgments and links the official asset/project, following its additional request. Images are original and unchanged. Unciv's banner is small and presented contained; Naev's title screenshot is credited as development media. Trademarks identify their creators and do not imply a partnership with F7.

Incomplete alternatives stay in review: Thrive, Widelands, Mindustry, Taisei, Open Surge, SuperTux, Breezy Weather, KDE Connect and Markor. Reasons include unclear artwork permission, conflicting license wording and blocked access. They are not published discoveries.
